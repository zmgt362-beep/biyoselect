# BIYOSELECT Decision Rules

## Priority

1. Expected net profit
2. Probability of success
3. Time to first measurable result
4. Net profit per hour
5. Reproducibility
6. Scalability
7. Cost
8. Risk
9. Difficulty
10. Opportunity cost

## Content selection

Prefer topics with a clear purchase decision, identifiable alternatives, measurable affiliate path, and enough trustworthy product information to make a defensible recommendation.

Reject or defer topics when the commercial path is unclear, evidence quality is poor, compliance risk is high, or expected profit per hour is materially below active alternatives.

## Recommendation integrity

The system must distinguish:

- verified fact
- source-derived data
- calculated metric
- heuristic judgment
- unknown

Unknown values must not be presented as verified facts.

## Experiment decisions

- Continue: signal is positive and economically plausible.
- Improve: signal exists but a specific bottleneck is identifiable.
- Stop: weak economics, weak signal, excessive effort, or unacceptable risk.

Avoid premature conclusions from tiny samples; record sample size and observation period.
