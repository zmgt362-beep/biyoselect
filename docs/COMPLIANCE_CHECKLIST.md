# BIYOSELECT Compliance Checklist

## Before publication

- Confirm advertising/affiliate disclosure requirements for the page and platform.
- Confirm the affiliate program permits the intended traffic source and link placement.
- Avoid misleading superiority claims.
- Avoid guaranteed or absolute efficacy claims without valid substantiation.
- Do not present cosmetic products as medicines.
- Identify material commercial relationships where required.
- Confirm prices and commercial conditions before displaying them as current.

## Before SNS publication

- Check the platform-specific affiliate/advertising rules.
- Check disclosure/PR labeling requirements.
- Confirm destination URLs and tracking behavior.
- Avoid copy that implies personal experience unless it is genuinely based on that experience.

## Operational rule

When the applicable rule is uncertain, stop the risky publication path and verify the primary source before proceeding.
